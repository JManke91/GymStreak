- The first-run tour no longer ends with a subscription offer. A fresh install goes straight from the last slide into the app.
- New: training reminders. Plan a routine and Gym Streak can remind you on the morning of each planned session. Right after the tour you get one screen offering it — saying no costs nothing and asks iOS for nothing.
- Reminders are strictly limited: at most one a day and three a week across every kind, and never while you are in the middle of a workout.
- New: a gentle nudge when you drift. If a planned session passes with nothing logged, you get one reminder the next morning. Without a plan, you get one after four days without a workout and at most one a week after that. Finishing a workout cancels any pending nudge.
- New: build a routine by describing it. Tap "Build a routine" in the Coach, type something like "Push day: bench press 3 sets of 8 at 60 kg, incline dumbbell press 3 by 10 at 22 kg", and the Coach drafts it for you. Review it, tap Create, and it is in your Routines and on your watch.
- The draft only ever uses exercises that are already in your library. Anything it could not match is listed as left out, never silently dropped and never invented. Discarding the draft, or closing the sheet, writes nothing.
- Fixed: exercise name matching no longer confuses a compound name with the shorter one inside it. Asking for "Schrägbankdrücken" no longer finds "Bankdrücken" (and the Coach no longer answers an incline-press PR question with your flat-bench numbers).
