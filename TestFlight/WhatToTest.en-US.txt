- The first-run tour no longer ends with a subscription offer. A fresh install goes straight from the last slide into the app.
- New: training reminders. Plan a routine and Gym Streak can remind you on the morning of each planned session. Right after the tour you get one screen offering it — saying no costs nothing and asks iOS for nothing.
- Reminders are strictly limited: at most one a day and three a week across every kind, and never while you are in the middle of a workout.
- New: a gentle nudge when you drift. If a planned session passes with nothing logged, you get one reminder the next morning. Without a plan, you get one after four days without a workout and at most one a week after that. Finishing a workout cancels any pending nudge.
- New: build a routine by describing it. Tap "Build a routine" in the Coach, type something like "Push day: bench press 3 sets of 8 at 60 kg, incline dumbbell press 3 by 10 at 22 kg", and the Coach drafts it for you. Review it, tap Create, and it is in your Routines and on your watch.
- The draft only ever uses exercises that are already in your library — nothing is silently dropped and nothing is invented. Discarding the draft, or closing the sheet, writes nothing.
- New: put right an exercise the draft could not place. It now stays in the draft as a row you can tap: pick the exercise you actually meant from your library and it keeps the sets, reps and weight you described, or remove the row outright. If the name matched several of your exercises, the picker offers those first. Rows you leave unanswered are left out when you tap Create, and the draft says so beforehand. Putting one right costs nothing against your free Coach requests.
- Fixed: exercise name matching no longer confuses a compound name with the shorter one inside it. Asking for "Schrägbankdrücken" no longer finds "Bankdrücken" (and the Coach no longer answers an incline-press PR question with your flat-bench numbers).
- New: Conditioning for fighters. Tap the boxing figure on the Routines screen to pick an interval or aerobic session (aerobic base, lactic intervals, alactic power), choose run, assault bike, rower, swim or sled/ropes, and follow the timer: phase, big countdown, round and effort cue, with a sound, a vibration and a 3-2-1 before every work interval. Lock the phone and the cues arrive as notifications. Each finished session is saved to Apple Health as its own workout.
- Conditioning sessions now land in your History. A finished session appears as its own card between your workouts, with the date, the machine, the time and the rounds you completed. Tap it for the details, or long-press to delete it — including from Apple Health when it was saved there.
