- Import your workout history from Strong: Settings → Import from Strong. Choose the CSV file Strong exported, check the preview, and your past workouts appear in History, progress charts and records. Importing the same file again adds nothing twice.
- When importing, you choose whether new exercises are added to your library. If you leave them out, the workouts still appear in History, and they join an exercise's progress chart once you add an exercise with the same name.
- When importing, you can pick a period, e.g. only the last month, or set your own start and end date.
- Import your workout history from Hevy: Settings → Import from Hevy. Choose the CSV file Hevy exported. The weight unit is read from the file, and warm-up sets are left out.
- Apple Watch: after finishing a workout, the summary shows whether it has reached your iPhone. It shows "Saving to iPhone…" and then "Saved on iPhone ✓". If your iPhone is out of reach, it says the workout is safe on the watch and will sync when the iPhone is nearby.
- Fight conditioning: log a mat or sparring session. In the Conditioning screen, tap "Log a mat session" and choose the sport, start time, duration and intensity. It appears in History with its own card, can be deleted, and is saved to Apple Health as martial arts, boxing or wrestling. It does not count toward your conditioning program week.
- Apple Watch: record a mat or sparring session live. On the routine list, tap "Mat session" and pick the sport. The watch shows elapsed time and heart rate. When you end, it suggests the intensity from your heart-rate zones, and you can change it with one tap. The session is saved to Apple Health and appears in your iPhone History.
- First launch: the tour now ends by asking how you train. "I lift" starts you in the app as before; "I lift and fight" opens the Fighter Strength program guide, which links on to fight conditioning. Nothing is added until you tap "Add program". Coming from Strong or Hevy? The same step links straight to the import.
- Round timer for sparring and class rounds: Conditioning → Round timer. Presets for BJJ (5 min rounds / 1 min rest), MMA (5/1), boxing (3/1) and Muay Thai (3/2), or your own round length, rest, round count — or open-ended. A bell starts and ends every round, a clacker warns 10 seconds before the end, and the bell rings even with the phone on silent. With the screen locked, each round arrives as a notification. Afterwards you can save the rounds as a mat session, pre-filled with the sport and the time — or just close the timer; on its own it leaves nothing in History.
- Apple Watch: the round timer runs on the watch too, without the iPhone, with haptics for round start, the warning and round end, and your live heart rate. On the routine list, tap "Round timer". At the end, save it as a mat session with the suggested intensity, or don't save it.
- History: a workout's detail now shows supersets grouped, with the same letter and colour they had during the workout. This applies to recorded workouts and to Hevy-imported ones with supersets.
- Round timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island: while the rounds run, the Lock Screen shows the time left for the whole session and a progress bar, and — while the app is open — the current round or rest with its own countdown. With the phone locked, the round detail steps back at the end of that round instead of showing a wrong one, and the overall time stays right. Pause freezes it, and at the end it shows your rounds and time for a moment.
